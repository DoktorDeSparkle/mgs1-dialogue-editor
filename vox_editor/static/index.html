    USA reference

      Shortcuts & workflow

      • Click a JPN line to select it, Shift-click another to select the range, ⌘/Ctrl-click to add or remove a line, Esc to clear. G (or Group) joins every chunk the selection touches into one chunk (one thought); join ↓ on a chunk joins it with the next one.
      • Grouping and U (ungroup) re-translate the new chunks with every engine, since the MT of the separate lines no longer fits. Subtitles you edited or re-timed are kept as they are (only the suggestions are refreshed; press use to replace them). One ⌘Z restores the chunks as they were before.
      • T translates the focused chunk with every configured engine; each engine's suggestion shows with a use button that puts it into the subtitle box (auto-split over the JPN timing, or into the marked subtitle when the chunk has several), where you edit it. "in subs" marks the suggestion the subtitles currently match.
      • Sort by MT engines disagree most to review first the lines where the models read the Japanese differently (≠ badge in the list).
      • Subtitle boxes: Enter = line break inside the subtitle, ⌘Enter = split at cursor into a new subtitle.
      • ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z undo/redo (structural edits and text commits).
      • [ / ] previous / next conversation in the current list.
      • Focusing a chunk highlights the USA lines LaBSE aligns it with. → on a USA line replaces the text of the target subtitle (the last one you clicked into, marked with a blue bar) and keeps its timing; if the chunk has no target, its subtitles become that one line over the JPN timing. ⇧→ appends it as a new subtitle instead.
      • Edits autosave to vox_editor_state.json. Export writes the same [text, timing] JSON format into exports/; untouched conversations and un-subtitled chunks keep their original JPN text.
