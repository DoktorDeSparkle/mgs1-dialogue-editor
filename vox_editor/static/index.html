      Shortcuts & workflow

      • Click a JPN line to select, Shift-click to extend, G to group into one chunk (one thought).
      • U ungroups the focused chunk. T translates it with every configured engine; the primary engine's output becomes the draft, the others show underneath with a use button.
      • Sort by MT engines disagree most to review first the lines where the models read the Japanese differently (≠ badge in the list).
      • Subtitle boxes: Enter = line break inside the subtitle, ⌘Enter = split at cursor into a new subtitle.
      • ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z undo/redo (structural edits and text commits).
      • [ / ] previous / next conversation in the current list.
      • Focusing a chunk highlights the USA lines LaBSE aligns it with; → on a USA line copies it into the focused chunk as a subtitle.
      • Edits autosave to vox_editor_state.json. Export writes a voxText-jpn-format file; untouched conversations and un-subtitled chunks keep their original JPN text.
